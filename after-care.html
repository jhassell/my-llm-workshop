Reset the Maintenance Reminder

The 2012 Frontier shows a wrench icon or "MAINTENANCE" message based on mileage. After an oil change:

  1. Turn ignition ON (push-button: press twice without brake; key: turn to ON). Do not start.
  2. Press the trip/reset knob (left of speedometer) until "SETTING" appears.
  3. Hold the knob ~3 seconds until "MAINTENANCE" appears.
  4. Press knob to select "OIL CHANGE" → press to select "RESET" → hold knob until it reads "0" or "COMPLETE."
  5. Turn ignition OFF. Start engine — wrench icon should be gone.

If your truck has the navigation/infotainment system, the reset may be in Settings → Maintenance on the touchscreen instead.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use 5W-20 or 10W-30 instead?

Stick with 5W-30. The VQ40DE was designed for 5W-30. Thinner oil (5W-20) reduces film strength at high temps, especially when towing. Thicker (10W-30) flows poorly on cold starts. Full synthetic 5W-30 is the right call year-round.

How often should I really change it?

Normal driving: 5,000 miles / 6 months.
Severe service (towing, dusty roads, short trips, idling): 3,000 miles / 3 months.
The truck's reminder uses an algorithm — it doesn't know if you're towing. Use the severe schedule if in doubt.

Do I need to prime the filter?

No. The cartridge housing fills from the engine's oil passages when you start. Pre-filling makes a mess and doesn't help. Just coat the O-rings with clean oil.

What if I overfilled by half a quart?

Drain the excess. Overfill aerates the oil (crankshaft whips it into foam), starving bearings. Pull the drain plug, let ~½ quart out, re-torque, recheck dipstick.

Can I reuse the crush washer?

No. They're $0.50. A reused washer leaks or seizes. Always replace.

My filter housing cap is stuck. What now?

Use a 19 mm six-point socket on a ½″ drive breaker bar. Steady pressure — no impact gun. If it won't budge, warm the engine 2 minutes (metal expands), then try. Penetrating oil on threads 10 min prior helps. Never use a pipe wrench on the housing body.

Where's the oil pressure sender? I want to add a gauge.

Passenger side of the block, near the oil filter housing. 1/8″ NPT. But the factory idiot light sender is just a switch — it won't drive a real gauge. For a real gauge, use a sandwich adapter at the filter housing or a remote sender.

What about the differential / transfer case / transmission fluid?

Different intervals. Rear diff: 30k miles (75W-90 GL-5). 4WD front diff: 30k. Transfer case: 60k (ATF Matic D/J). Transmission: 60k (NS-2 CVT fluid if CVT, Matic S if 5-speed auto). This guide covers engine oil only.

Record Keeping

Log it: date, mileage, oil brand/viscosity, filter brand. A notebook in the glove box or a phone app (myCARFAX, Drivvo, Fuelly) works. When you sell the truck, a complete history adds value.
