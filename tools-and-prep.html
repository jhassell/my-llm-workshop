Quick reminder: The 2012 Frontier 4.0L uses a cartridge filter in a metal housing, not a spin-on canister. You need a 19 mm socket or cap wrench for the filter housing — a standard oil filter wrench won't fit.

Preparation (Do This First)

  1. Warm the engine. Run it 3–5 minutes if cold. Warm oil drains faster and carries more contaminants. Don't run it hot — you want it warm, not scorching.
  2. Park on level ground. Set parking brake. Chock the rear wheels.
  3. Lift the front. Jack up the front crossmember or frame rails. Secure on jack stands at the frame pinch points. Shake the truck to confirm it's stable.
  4. Remove the skid plate (if equipped). Four 12 mm bolts hold the plastic engine cover/skid plate. Set bolts aside in a magnetic tray.
  5. Pop the hood. Remove the oil filler cap (on the valve cover) and pull the dipstick halfway out. This lets air in so oil drains freely.
  6. Position the drain pan. Slide it under the oil pan drain plug — slightly forward of center since oil arcs backward as it drains.

Parts Layout (Mental Checklist)

Before you crack the drain plug, verify you have:

  • ☐ New crush washer on the drain plug
  • ☐ New filter cartridge + O-rings (usually included in the box)
  • ☐ 5.4 quarts of 5W-30 synthetic ready to pour
  • ☐ Torque wrench set to 30 ft-lb (drain) and 15 ft-lb (filter cap)
