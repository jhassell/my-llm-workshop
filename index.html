Changing the Oil in a 2012 Nissan Frontier

A four-page guide for the 4.0L V6 (VQ40DE) engine

Why This Guide?

The 2012 Nissan Frontier with the 4.0L V6 is a reliable truck, but it needs regular oil changes — every 5,000 miles or 6 months under normal driving, or every 3,000 miles if you tow, haul, or drive in dusty conditions.

This guide walks you through the job in four short pages:

  1. Tools required — what you need (and don't need)
  2. Parts, oil, filter & preparation — what to buy and how to get ready
  3. Step-by-step procedure — drain, filter swap, refill, and cleanup
  4. After-care & common questions — torque specs, reset the maintenance light, disposal, and FAQs

Quick Reference

ItemSpecification
Engine4.0L V6 (VQ40DE)
Oil capacity (with filter)5.4 quarts (5.1 L)
Oil type5W-30 synthetic (API SN/SP, ILSAC GF-5/6)
Oil filterNissan 15208-31U00 or equivalent (Wix 57047, Fram PH3600, Purolator L14610)
Drain plug14 mm, 25–33 ft-lb (34–45 N·m)
Filter housing19 mm cap, ~15 ft-lb (20 N·m) — do not overtighten

Safety First

  • Work on level ground, parking brake set, wheels chocked.
  • Let the engine cool 10–15 minutes if hot — warm oil drains faster, but hot oil burns.
  • Use jack stands if you raise the truck; never rely on the jack alone.
  • Wear nitrile gloves and safety glasses.
