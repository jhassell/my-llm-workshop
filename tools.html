Nice to Have

  • Ramps (instead of jack/stands) — quicker, but you still need wheel chocks
  • Creepers — saves your back and clothes
  • Headlamp — hands-free light under the truck
  • Torque angle gauge — if you like precision on the filter cap
  • Oil extractor (suction pump) — for top-down changes, but drain plug is faster on this truck

What You Don't Need

  • Standard spin-on oil filter wrench — the VQ40DE uses a cartridge filter in a metal housing; strap/cap wrenches for canister filters won't fit
  • Impact gun — never use on drain plug or filter housing (cracks aluminum)
  • Oil filter pliers — same reason; wrong tool for cartridge housing
  • Specialty Nissan tools — everything is standard metric sizes

Tool Kit Shopping List (If Starting Fresh)

Minimal viable kit (~$80–120 total):

  1. 14 mm + 19 mm 6-point sockets (⅜″ drive)
  2. ⅜″ ratchet + 6″ extension
  3. Click torque wrench 10–50 ft-lb (~$35–60)
  4. Low-profile 6+ qt drain pan (~$15)
  5. 2-ton floor jack + 2 jack stands (~$60–100)
  6. Wheel chocks (~$10)
  7. Nitrile gloves, safety glasses, funnel, rags (~$20)

Borrow the jack/stands if you don't have them — many auto parts stores loan them free with a deposit.
